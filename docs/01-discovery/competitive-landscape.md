# Competitive Landscape

> ASSUMPTION: competitor categories and behaviors below are inferred from the home-services / sport-court market generally. Validate specific competitor URLs and tactics with local SERP research before the pitch.

## Competitor categories

### 1. National franchise / brand builders
Examples (category, not confirmed local presence): **Sport Court** (the brand), **VersaCourt**, **PaceMaster / modular tile** vendors.

- **Strengths:** strong brand, national ad budgets, polished sites, product catalogs, dealer locators.
- **Weaknesses:** generic national pages with weak per-city local relevance; lead handoff to local dealers is slow; little personalization.
- **Where GRIT wins:** hyper-local city pages + the personalized Backyard Previewer + sub-60s speed-to-lead.

### 2. Local/regional contractors (direct competitors)
Other Wasatch Front court and concrete/flatwork contractors.

- **Strengths:** local trust, word of mouth, Angi/HomeAdvisor presence.
- **Weaknesses:** typically the same brochure-site problem GRIT has today — no city pages, no schema, dead-end forms, no attribution.
- **Where GRIT wins:** simply doing the SEO + conversion + speed-to-lead trifecta well leapfrogs most of them.

### 3. Aggregators / marketplaces
**Angi, HomeAdvisor, Thumbtack, Houzz.**

- **Reality:** these rank well and currently supply GRIT leads, but they commoditize and resell the same lead to competitors and charge per lead.
- **Where GRIT wins:** owned organic city pages produce leads GRIT doesn't pay per-click for and doesn't share. The goal is to shift the lead mix toward owned channels over time, reducing dependence on paid aggregators.

### 4. Meta-ad competitors
Anyone running the same Facebook/Instagram ad formats GRIT runs.

- **Where GRIT wins:** server-side CAPI gives Meta cleaner conversion signal → lower CPL than competitors relying on degraded pixel-only data; the Previewer is a scroll-stopping creative hook.

## Competitive matrix

| Capability | National brands | Local contractors | Aggregators | **GRIT (rebuilt)** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Per-city local pages | Weak | Rare | Strong | **Strong** |
| Schema / rich results | Partial | Rare | Strong | **Strong** |
| Interactive estimator | Sometimes | Rare | N/A | **Yes** |
| Photo-personalized render | No | No | No | **Yes (differentiator)** |
| Speed-to-lead < 60s | Slow handoff | Varies | N/A | **Yes** |
| Server-side attribution | Varies | Rare | Yes | **Yes** |
| Mobile performance | Good | Varies | Good | **Lighthouse ≥95** |

## Strategic takeaways

1. The **Backyard Previewer is a genuine moat** — no category competitor offers photo-personalized renders. Lead with it in the pitch and in ad creative.
2. **Local SEO is an open lane** against direct local competitors who haven't done it.
3. **CAPI is a quiet edge** — better ad efficiency than competitors on degraded pixel data.
4. **Don't fight aggregators on their turf** — build the owned channel and shift mix over time.
