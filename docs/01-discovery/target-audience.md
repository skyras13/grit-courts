# Target Audience

> ASSUMPTION: personas below are inferred from the product (high-ticket residential sport courts on the Wasatch Front) and typical buyer profiles. Validate against the owner's actual closed-deal data.

## Who they are (in one line)

Affluent suburban Utah homeowners with a yard, kids or an active lifestyle, and the disposable income to spend $18k–$45k turning their backyard into a destination.

## Primary persona — "Active-family homeowner" (Megan & Tyler)

- **Demographics:** 35–50, married, 2–4 kids, household income $150k+, owns a single-family home in a Wasatch Front suburb (Draper, Alpine, Highland, Lehi, Lindon, Cedar Hills).
- **Home:** $600k–$1.2M, decent-sized backyard, often new-ish construction with room to build.
- **Triggers:** kids got into pickleball/basketball; want to keep teens home; "staycation" backyard upgrade; neighbor got one; finished a basement/pool and the court is next.
- **Mindset:** treats it as a home-improvement + family-lifestyle investment, not a toy. Wants quality, durability, and a result that looks high-end (resale + status).
- **Fears:** getting an ugly slab; contractor who disappears; surprise costs; "will it even fit / look good in MY yard?"
- **Where they are:** on their phone, scrolling Facebook/Instagram, googling at night after the kids are down.

> The Backyard Previewer directly answers their single biggest fear — *will it look good in my yard?* — which is why it's the flagship.

## Secondary persona — "Pickleball enthusiast" (Dave)

- 45–65, recently obsessed with pickleball, plays at the rec center and wants his own court, budget flexibility, decisive once convinced. Highly responsive to a court rendered into his actual yard.

## Tertiary persona — "Resale/luxury upgrader" (Sara)

- 40–60, upgrading the home for lifestyle and resale value, cares about aesthetics and how it photographs, multi-sport or basketball common. Responsive to gallery quality and design polish.

## What they need from the site

| Need | How we serve it |
|---|---|
| "Will it fit/look good in my yard?" | **Backyard Previewer** |
| "Roughly what does this cost?" | **Estimator price range** |
| "Are these people legit?" | Social proof: 4.8★, HBA membership, gallery, local testimonials |
| "Do they work in my area?" | **City pages** with local relevance |
| "I want this handled, fast" | Sub-60s speed-to-lead response |
| Mobile-first, at night, one-handed | Lighthouse ≥95, big tap targets, fast |

## Implications for the build

- **Mobile-first, fast** — they're on phones. (Lighthouse ≥95, CWV good.)
- **Visual and aspirational** — galleries, big imagery, the render. Navy (#2b598a) premium feel.
- **Low-friction qualification** — estimator before asking for everything; capture lead even if they bail mid-render.
- **Trust everywhere** — ratings, HBA, real local testimonials per city.
- **Speed-to-lead** — they expect a near-instant response like any premium service.
